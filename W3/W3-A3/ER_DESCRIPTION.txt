1. Additional Attributes:
 I would add the following attributes:
 i. Student: Email; to store the student's email address.
 ii. Student: Phone_number; to store the student's contact number.
 iii. Enrollment: Enrollment_status; to show whether the enrollment is 'active', 'completed', or 'cancelled'.

2. Relationships Between Entities:
 
 i. Student – Enrollment:
 The relationship is Enrolls. A student can have many enrollments, because they can enroll in multiple courses. Each enrollment belongs to a student. Therefore, this can be considered a one-to-many (1) relationship from Student to Enrollment.
 ii. Enrollment – Lecture:
 An enrollment is associated with the lectures related to the course or subject. A lecture can be attended by multiple enrolled students, while a student can attend multiple lectures. Therefore, this can be considered a many-to-many (M) relationship.
 iii. Lecturer – Lecture:
 A lecturer can conduct many lectures, while each lecture is normally conducted by a lecturer. Therefore, this is a one-to-many (1) relationship from Lecturer to Lecture.
 iv. Subjects – Lecture:
 A subject can have many lectures, while each lecture belongs to a particular subject. Therefore, this is a one-to-many (1) relationship from Subjects to Lecture.
 v. Lecturer – Subjects:
 The Lectures relationship connects lecturers and subjects. A lecturer can teach multiple subjects, and a subject can be taught by multiple lecturers. Therefore, this can be considered a many-to-many (M) relationship, with Lecture acting as the connection between them.
